# VK Finance: family user guide

VK Finance keeps your family's money in one place: accounts, spending, budgets, bills, loans and
reports. Everyone signs in with their own username, and each person sees only what their role
allows. Another family never sees anything of yours.

The same answers are inside the app under **Help**.

## 1. Signing in

1. Open the portal link your Family Admin sent you.
2. Type the **username** and **temporary password** they gave you.
3. Choose your own password straight away. It needs at least 10 characters (12 for admins), with a
   mix of upper case, lower case, numbers or symbols, and can't contain your name or username.

The temporary password works for 24 hours. After that, ask your Family Admin for a new one.

**Staying safe while signed in**

- You're signed out after 30 minutes without activity, and after 12 hours at most.
- On a shared computer, always choose **Sign out** when you finish.
- **Security** shows every device you're signed in on. Sign out any you don't recognise.

**Forgot your password?** Choose *Forgot your password?* on the sign-in page. If your account has an
email address, you'll get a link that works for 30 minutes. Otherwise ask your Family Admin to
reset it.

**Locked out?** Five wrong attempts lock the account for 30 minutes (longer if it keeps happening).
Wait, or ask your Family Admin to unlock you from the **Family** page.

## 2. Two-step verification

Two-step verification adds a second lock. After your password, you also type a 6-digit code from
an app on your phone, so someone who learns your password still can't get in. **Family Admins and
Co-Admins should always turn it on.**

**Turning it on**

1. Install an authenticator app on your phone, such as Google Authenticator or Microsoft
   Authenticator.
2. In VK Finance, open **Security** and choose **Set up**.
3. Type your password, then scan the QR code with the app. If you can't scan, choose *Enter a key
   instead* and type the key into the app.
4. Type the 6-digit code the app shows.
5. **Save your 10 recovery codes.** Download or copy them and keep them somewhere safe, away from
   your phone.

**Signing in with two-step:** after your password, open the app and type the code it shows for VK
Finance. Codes change every 30 seconds, so use the newest one.

**No phone with you?** Choose *Use a recovery code* and type one of your saved codes. Each works
once. When you're running low, make a fresh set from **Security**.

**Lost your phone and your recovery codes?** Ask your Family Admin to choose **Reset two-step** for
you on the Family page. You can then sign in with your password and set it up again.

**Code always wrong?** Make sure your phone sets its date and time automatically.

## 3. Recording money

- **Add a transaction:** choose **Add transaction**, or press **N** on a keyboard. Pick *Expense*,
  *Income* or *Transfer*, then the amount, the account and a category.
- **Moving money between your own accounts** (for example paying the credit card from your bank) is
  a *Transfer*, so it's not counted as spending twice.
- **Mistakes:** open the entry to edit it, or *void* it. A voided entry stays on record but no
  longer counts. Children can change their own entries for 24 hours.
- **Find anything** on the Transactions page by month, type, account, category, person or search
  text, and export what you see to CSV.

## 4. Accounts and cards

Add everything that holds or owes money: bank accounts, cash, UPI wallets, credit cards, loans,
fixed deposits and investments. Enter the balance on the day you start as the *opening balance*.
After that, VK Finance keeps every balance up to date. It also re-checks each account every night
and corrects any difference.

> **Never type a full card number, CVV, PIN or bank password anywhere in VK Finance.** It only ever
> needs the last 4 digits of a card, so you can tell your cards apart.

**Fixed and recurring deposits:** choose **Fixed Deposit** or **Recurring Deposit** and enter the
terms from the receipt: the FD/RD number, the amount invested (or monthly instalment), the rate,
the date invested and the duration. VK Finance works out the maturity date and amount the way
Indian banks do. FDs compound quarterly, and terms under 6 months earn simple interest. RD
instalments compound quarterly to maturity. If the receipt shows a different maturity amount,
enter it and the bank's figure is used. You can also record the nominee, senior citizen rate,
interest payout (monthly, quarterly, half-yearly, yearly or at maturity), tax-saver FD (5-year
lock-in) and Form 15G/15H. The full deposit number is shown only to the owner and admins.

- Link an RD to the account its instalments come from, and each month's instalment is recorded
  for you. A payout FD records its interest into the account you choose.
- You get a reminder a week before a deposit matures. Then use **Record maturity** (or **Close
  early**, which applies the bank's penalty) to book the interest as income and move the money.
  Or use **Renew** to roll the FD over for a new term, with or without the interest.
- The **Deposits** panel shows what you've invested, its value today, its value at maturity and
  this financial year's interest. It warns you when interest from one bank is likely to attract
  TDS (above ₹50,000 a year, or ₹1,00,000 for senior citizens). It also warns when one
  person's deposits at one bank go beyond the ₹5 lakh DICGC insurance cover.

**Credit cards:** besides the limit, statement day, days to pay and minimum due %, you can record
the network (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay, Amex, Diners), the *valid thru* month, the cash withdrawal
limit, the finance charge (% a month), the annual fee and the yearly spend that waives it, and the
bank autopay you have set up. Never enter the full card number or the CVV. Each card shows its
interest-free period, how much more spend waives the fee, and when it expires. Under RBI rules,
no late fee applies until a bill is more than 3 days overdue. Edit a card any time with the pencil
on its tile.

**Cards sharing one bank limit:** a Family Admin or Co-Admin can open **Accounts → Shared credit limits → Create shared limit**, enter the bank-confirmed facility limit and choose its cards. Availability uses the combined balances, while each card keeps its own bill and due date. The overview counts that shared limit once. Grouping never moves money. Archiving a group restores each card's saved independent limit. Shared totals are private when you cannot see every linked card.

## 5. Budgets, bills, loans and regular payments

- **Budgets:** set an amount per category for the month. A bar turns amber when spending is running
  ahead of the month and red when it's over. Admins get a notice at 80% and 100%.
- **Bills:** add each bill once with its due day. VK Finance creates it every month, reminds you
  before it's due, and can pay it automatically from an account you choose.
- **Loans and EMIs:** add the loan's amount, rate and tenure. You'll see each EMI split into
  principal and interest, the outstanding balance and the date you'll be debt-free. Mark it
  fixed or floating, and enter the EMI and outstanding from your bank statement if they differ
  from the calculated ones. When the bank changes the rate, use **Rate changed** on the loan:
  choose whether the bank changed the EMI or the number of EMIs left. Every change is kept in
  the loan's rate history.
  Use **Edit** on a loan to fix its details (name, lender, loan account number, type, borrower,
  EMI account) or to correct its terms to match the bank's statement. Corrected terms re-price
  the loan. A loan added by mistake can be deleted until a payment is recorded against it. RBI
  rules don't allow banks to charge a prepayment or foreclosure fee on floating-rate loans to
  individuals.
- **Recurring entries** (salary, rent, SIPs, pocket money) can post themselves, or ask you first.
- **The bell** (top left) collects reminders, overdue alerts and security notices.

## 6. Reports

Reports offers 13 views of the family's money, including the monthly summary, spending by category,
budget against actual, cash-flow forecast, credit cards, loans, bills and the annual summary. Some
are for admins only.

- Click a category to see its sub-categories, then the individual entries.
- **Export CSV** opens in Excel or Google Sheets. **Print** has a *Save as PDF* option.
- **Save** keeps a report with your filters, and you can share it with the family.

## 7. For Family Admins

| Role | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| Family Admin | Everything, including managing other admins |
| Co-Admin | Everything except managing Family Admins |
| Member | Their own accounts and shared ones |
| Child | Their own accounts; can change their own entries for 24 hours |
| Viewer | Look but not change (their own money, or the whole family's) |

- **Add a member** on the Family page. Give them their username and the temporary password, which is
  shown only once.
- **Unlock, reset a password, reset two-step or sign someone out** from the **⋯** menu next to
  their name.
- **Sensitive changes need your password again**, plus a code if you use two-step. These are role
  changes, resets and removing members. This stops anyone from using your unlocked device to make
  them.
- **Someone left the family?** Choose *Remove from family*. Their sign-in and secrets are deleted,
  and the transactions they recorded stay in your history.
- You're told automatically when a family member's account gets locked.

## 8. Appearance

Open **Appearance** from the menu to make VK Finance look the way you like. Every change previews
straight away; press **Save** to keep it, or **Discard** to go back.

- **Mode:** *Light*, *Dark*, or *Match device* to follow your phone or computer. The sun or moon
  button at the top of every page switches light and dark in one tap.
- **Colour theme:** nine themes (VK Teal, Ocean, Indigo, Grape, Berry, Sunset, Gold, Forest and
  Graphite) or *Custom* for any colour. If a colour is too light for white button text, VK Finance
  darkens buttons slightly so they stay easy to read. Account card colours stay as you chose them.
- **Corners** (*Sharp*, *Soft* or *Round*) and **Text size** (*Standard* or *Large*).
- **Family theme:** Family Admins and Co-Admins can set a family default under *Family default*,
  and can stop members choosing their own colours. Light or dark and text size always stay each
  person's own choice. Members can pick *Family theme* to keep following whatever the admin sets.

Your choice is saved to your sign-in, so it follows you to every device.

## 9. Privacy and safety

- Passwords are never stored as text. They're salted and hashed thousands of times, so nobody can
  read them, including the people who run VK Finance.
- Your family's data is separated from every other family's, and every sign-in and change is
  recorded.
- **If something looks wrong:**
  1. Change your password.
  2. Sign out your other devices.
  3. Turn on two-step verification.
  4. Tell your Family Admin.
